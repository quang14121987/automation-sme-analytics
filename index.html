Privacy Policy — Automation SME Analytics

Effective date: September 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Automation SME Analytics is an application developed for managing and analyzing content published through LinkedIn.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be accessed and used when the application interacts with LinkedIn APIs.

2. Information We Access

Depending on the permissions granted to the application and the functionality being used, Automation SME Analytics may access information related to LinkedIn content and analytics associated with the application's authorized LinkedIn account.

This may include:

  • LinkedIn post identifiers;
  • post publication information;
  • post analytics and engagement metrics;
  • information returned by LinkedIn APIs that is necessary to provide the application's functionality.

The application does not request information that is unrelated to its intended functionality.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through LinkedIn APIs is used only for purposes related to:

  • retrieving analytics for LinkedIn posts;
  • monitoring post performance;
  • storing and analyzing post performance data;
  • generating reports and analytics for the application owner.

The information is not used for unrelated purposes.

4. Data Storage

Information obtained through LinkedIn APIs may be stored in the application's database for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

The application owner is responsible for maintaining appropriate technical and organizational safeguards for stored information.

5. Data Sharing

Information obtained through LinkedIn APIs is not intentionally sold or shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

Information may be processed by technical service providers used to operate the application when such processing is necessary to provide the application's functionality.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy or as otherwise required by applicable obligations.

When information is no longer required, reasonable steps may be taken to delete or anonymize it.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information handled by the application against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. LinkedIn

Automation SME Analytics uses LinkedIn APIs and is subject to LinkedIn's applicable policies and terms.

LinkedIn is a separate service provider, and information handled by LinkedIn is also subject to LinkedIn's own privacy practices.
